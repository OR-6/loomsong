# Quest test: what to do and what to send back

About 20 minutes. Quest 3 or 3S, hand tracking on, controllers put away, sitting at a real table with decent light.

**Link:** https://or-6.github.io/loomsong/?debug=1
The `?debug=1` part shows a small tag behind the picture card with three numbers: frame rate, draw calls and a quality level (`q0` is full quality; it drops to `q1`/`q2` if frames are missed). Please photograph or note those numbers at each step where it says **(fps)**.

## Steps

1. Open the link in the headset's browser and press **Enter mixed reality**. Accept any permission prompts.
   - Send: did it ask for anything unexpected? Did passthrough appear?
2. Rest one hand flat on the table, palm down, for a second.
   - Send: did the loom appear on the table, at the right height (not floating, not sunk in)? How long did it take? **(fps)**
3. Follow the hints without help from anyone. Weave the first picture (the Sprout).
   - Send: was it clear what to do? Where did you get stuck, and for how long? When "This row needs more threads" appeared, did you work out the second lever yourself? **(fps while weaving)**
   - Try both: pinch and slide a lever, and also just poke one with a fingertip.
4. Watch the plush come out of the cloth, dance and hop onto the shelf.
   - Send: could you see it well? Did the sound play? **(fps during the celebration)**
5. Weave the second picture (the Tulip). Then poke a plush on the shelf; pinch one and carry it onto the table somewhere, then let go.
   - Send: did it stay where you put it? Did anything feel hard to reach?
6. Poke the small wrapped present beside the yarn basket (the picture of the day) and weave it.
   - Send: did its plush land somewhere sensible on the table?
7. Run a fingertip across the threads like a harp.
   - Send: did each thread play? Any lag between touch and sound?
8. Take the headset off for a minute (or press the system button to leave), then come back to the same link.
   - Send: did the loom come back in the same place ("Right where you left it"), with the plushes where you left them?
9. Optional: open the same link with `?debug=1&shadows=0` and repeat step 3 briefly.
   - Send: the frame rate with and without shadows.
10. Optional, with a friend and a phone: after the first picture, poke the little red toy telephone on the desk and read the four-letter code on its tag to your friend. They open the same link on their phone and join with the code.
   - Send: did the picture disappear from the card in the headset? When your friend tapped a square, did a red felt pin appear on the right thread? Did "Ring Pip" make Pip wave? Did they get a postcard when the picture was finished?
11. Optional: open the link with `?finale=1` and place the loom. After a few seconds the loom weaves a heart by itself and the shelves sing in turn.
   - Send: **(fps during the finale)**, and whether the credit ribbon was readable from the seat.

12. Optional, if a Quest 2 is around: repeat steps 1–4 on it. The room shows in black and white there; the game stays in colour.
   - Send: **(fps)** while weaving and during the plush celebration, and whether pinches felt reliable.
13. Pick up a controller for a moment.
   - Send: did a tag ask you to put the controllers down? When you did, did your hands work again?

## Also send

- Headset model, and whether the room has a desk or table saved in Space Setup.
- Any moment the hands lost tracking or grabbed the wrong thing.
- Anything that looked broken, glitched, or hard to read from the seat.
- One sentence: what was the best moment, and what was the most confusing one?
