# Video shot list (real passthrough on a Quest 3)

Target length: **2:30** (judges may stop at 3:00). Real gameplay only, recorded in the headset.
Open on what only mixed reality can do: a plush landing on your real desk.

## Before you record

- **Desk:** clear and tidy, warm light, nothing branded in view (no logos on mugs, laptops or books).
  A plant or a lamp at the edge of the frame helps it read as a real room.
- **Headset:** charged, lenses clean, hand tracking on, controllers out of reach.
- **Recording:** press the Meta button, open **Camera**, choose **Record video**. In the camera
  settings pick landscape (16:9) and the highest resolution offered. Turn the microphone off: we
  add music and voice-over afterwards.
- **Move slowly.** Turn your head about half as fast as feels natural, and hold each shot for a
  beat before and after the action. Fast head turns look shaky in the recording.
- **Sound:** the game's music is Scott Buckley (CC BY); it can stay in the video. Credit it in the
  description: "Music: Scott Buckley, scottbuckley.com.au (CC BY 4.0)".
- **Jump-to links for filming** (each starts at that moment; they change saved progress, so record
  the fresh-start shots first):
  - fresh start: settings on the 2D page → "Move the loom", and clear the site's data in the browser
  - `https://or-6.github.io/loomsong/?start=friends`: the Table (Garden's fourth picture), with the Garden plushes on the shelf
  - `?start=lantern`: the Lantern at Night (the room dims)
  - `?start=boat`: the Boat by the Sea (light ripples on the table)
  - `?finale=1`: the grand finale a few seconds after the loom is out
  - `?sleep=1`: Pip asleep on the perch (welcome back)
